# Reusable appearance observation guide

2026-10-25. Approved bounded Phase 3 evidence contract. These optional observations
support the interview appearance heuristic; they are not professional certification.
They are reusable across future approved contexts and require no per-event tag grid.

Review the actual garment and its displayed category/image. Unknown is appropriate
when the image or your knowledge cannot establish a feature. No category, style name,
material description or AI confidence supplies an observation automatically.

| Field | Record Yes only when | Unknown / limits |
|---|---|---|
|Collared neckline|You identify a collar at the neckline.|A long-sleeve category alone says nothing about a collar. A folded/cropped image can hide it.|
|Tailored cut|You identify deliberate shaping from construction such as darts, panels or a defined waist.|This is your design judgement, not a measurement of quality, price, wearer fit or professional appropriateness.|
|Structured shape|You identify construction intended to hold a defined outline rather than only drape.|Do not infer hidden reinforcement from a style label or uncertain photo. Choose Unknown if uncertain.|
|Dated condition observation|You observed it clean and without visible damage, or observed that it needs attention.|This records your observation at that time, not continuous verification. Check current physical condition and wearer fit before wearing.|

These synthetic text examples validate the **contract**, not human visual accuracy:

| Example | Recorded evidence | Effect in the interview heuristic |
|---|---|---|
|A top with a collar the owner identifies|collared=true|May supply the upper-member feature; still needs reviewed smart-casual style and clean/intact observation.|
|Long-sleeve top, neckline not visible|collared=unknown|Unknown; no collar invented from category.|
|Owner identifies shaping in trousers|tailored=true|May supply the lower-member feature; no fabric quality/fit claim.|
|Owner cannot tell whether trousers are tailored|tailored=unknown|Unknown, even with a high-confidence AI suggestion.|
|Owner identifies a structured dress|structured=true|May supply the one-piece feature; style and dated condition remain separate.|
|Owner judges a dress not structured|structured=false|Unknown for this positive heuristic; not a universal claim that the dress is inappropriate.|
|Outerwear with a reviewed tailored cut but unknown structure|tailored=true, structured=unknown|May supply the outerwear feature via the approved OR, with all other conditions still mandatory.|
|Any garment recorded as needing attention|condition=needs_attention|Fails the bounded interview condition gate; another positive cannot erase it.|

Save only after explicitly reviewing the displayed facts. Cancel restores the saved
draft without writing. Withdraw removes only these appearance observations, preserving
style review and wedding choices. Failed saves retain drafts. Category changes
invalidate dependent observations; source-media changes make them stale. Style
changes invalidate style review independently. No old garments are backfilled.

No raw image assessment, user study, expert fashion validation or calibrated AI field
admission is claimed by these examples. Existing model-evidence dependencies remain.
